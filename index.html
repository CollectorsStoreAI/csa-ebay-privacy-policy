Privacy Policy

Collectors Store AI

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

Collectors Store AI is an owner-only internal application for managing the owner's collectibles inventory and eBay selling workflow. It is not a public SaaS product, marketplace, data broker, or service offered to other sellers or customers.

1. Operator and contact

Collectors Store AI ("CSA") is operated in Japan by its individual owner under the trading name Collectors Store Tokyo. The trading name does not state or imply that the operator is a corporation.

Privacy, deletion, and authorization questions may be sent to collectorsstoretokyo@gmail.com. Where applicable law requires additional operator identification to be made known on request, the owner will provide the required information without undue delay through an appropriate response process.

2. Scope and purposes

CSA uses eBay data and operator-provided information only to:

  • research active listings relevant to the owner's sourcing decisions;
  • identify and de-duplicate products and maintain the owner's inventory records;
  • prepare, review, create, and manage listings for items owned by the operator;
  • record sales entered manually by the owner and reconcile related inventory;
  • record listing, offer, and workflow status for auditing and operational safety;
  • if separately authorized and enabled in the future, manage order fulfillment using only necessary order data after the required safeguards and policy update are complete; and
  • meet applicable security, authorization, and eBay account-deletion obligations.

CSA does not automate bidding, purchasing, or checkout. Browse prices are not treated as completed-sale prices or proof of demand. Listing publication requires an explicit owner action and the required eBay authorization.

3. Data processed

Depending on the approved and enabled eBay API functions, CSA may process:

  • active-listing data such as item ID, title, price, currency, condition, category, shipping cost, buying options, item country, item and image URLs, search parameters, marketplace, and retrieval time;
  • owner inventory and listing data such as internal SKU, product identity, grading and condition information, photographs, quantity, cost status, storage location, listing title and description, item specifics, price, marketplace, merchant location key, business-policy IDs, offer ID, listing ID, and processing status;
  • application and authorization data such as eBay application identifiers, OAuth tokens, token-expiry metadata, and non-secret account configuration; and
  • limited operational records entered by the owner, including an eBay order identifier when the owner records a sale manually.

New Browse candidate records do not persist seller username or seller feedback merely for candidate discovery. Such fields may be observed transiently while processing an API response. CSA does not currently use an eBay Orders API and does not currently retrieve buyer name, email address, telephone number, postal address, payment details, or other buyer personal information through an eBay API.

Any future Orders API or buyer-data functionality will require separate authorization, data minimization, appropriate safeguards, and an updated privacy policy before use.

4. Sources and use of information

Information may come from:

  • eBay APIs authorized for the operator's eBay account;
  • information and photographs supplied directly by the owner;
  • the owner's manual Product Research workflow; and
  • other lawful sources identified by the owner inside CSA.

CSA does not use an eBay password, browser cookie, or private web endpoint for API access. It does not sell, license, publicly distribute, or use eBay data for third-party advertising, unrelated profiling, or a third-party research service.

5. Storage and security

  • Primary application records are stored locally in SQLite databases on the owner's Mac.
  • Managed product images are stored in owner-controlled local application folders.
  • Authorization credentials are kept in owner-controlled storage with restricted access and are not published or included in public policy pages.
  • The owner's iCloud Drive may be used to receive photographs from the owner's iPhone before local import.
  • If the owner enables the backup workflow, database backups and managed images may be copied to an owner-controlled Google Drive folder. Secrets, OAuth tokens, passwords, cookies, private keys, and authentication files are excluded from that backup design.
  • The private source-code repository is not used to store the live database, live product images, or application secrets.

CSA applies least privilege, separates Sandbox and Production settings, avoids logging authorization headers or tokens, and uses explicit human confirmation for sensitive selling actions. No security measure can eliminate every risk.

6. Service providers and disclosure

The application is not distributed to third parties. Access to application records is restricted to the owner and any service providers or support personnel specifically authorized for necessary operations, subject to applicable obligations.

Information may be processed by the following providers only when the related function is used:

  • eBay, for API authorization, listing research, listing operations, and applicable account-deletion handling;
  • Apple iCloud, when the owner uses iCloud Drive to receive item photographs;
  • Google Drive, when the owner enables the backup workflow;
  • Cloudflare, for the public eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notification endpoint and, once deployed, public privacy-policy hosting; and
  • GitHub, for private source-code storage without the live database or secrets.

CSA contains optional image-analysis functionality that can use the OpenAI API, but it is not currently used for this production eBay workflow. The owner will update this policy and complete the required approval and safeguards before enabling that processing for production item images.

These providers may process data outside Japan under their respective terms and privacy practices. CSA may also disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect the application and the owner's rights.

7. Retention and deletion

CSA keeps information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enabled purpose, applicable legal obligations, security, auditability, and eBay requirements. A fixed retention period has not yet been adopted for every record type. The owner reviews continued need and performs deletion manually where automated retention is not available.

The backup design targets daily and monthly recovery copies, but automatic pruning and deletion propagation across backup copies are not currently implemented. Deletion from backups therefore requires a separate manual review and propagation step.

CSA operates a public HTTPS service designed to receive eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications. After signature verification, the service is designed to store only minimal notification metadata such as the notification identifier, dates, attempt count, processing status, and timestamps; it does not persist the notification's username, user ID, or eIAS token. The endpoint is registered for Production Marketplace Account Deletion notifications. Registration does not by itself establish that the complete production deletion and backup-propagation workflow is operational; that end-to-end workflow remains subject to verification.

Where a valid deletion obligation or request from an affected individual or eBay applies, the owner will, without undue delay and no later than any earlier applicable eBay deadline, identify and remove the relevant eBay-derived cache and related records, subject to legal retention requirements, and perform the separate manual deletion review and propagation step for applicable backup copies. Requests may be sent to the privacy contact above.

8. Authorization and privacy choices

The owner may revoke CSA's eBay authorization through the applicable eBay account or developer settings. CSA will stop using revoked authorization and will review retained information for deletion or legally required retention. Do not send eBay passwords, OAuth tokens, API secrets, or authentication codes by email.

Information about eBay's own privacy practices and available privacy choices is provided in the eBay Privacy Notice.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CSA's functions, service providers, legal obligations, or eBay requirements change. The page will show the date of the latest update. Material changes will be reviewed before the related processing begins.
